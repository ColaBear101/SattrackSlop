    How these numbers were produced

    Elements

    Inclination, RAAN, eccentricity, argument of perigee and mean anomaly are read from their fixed columns in TLE line 2 — no fitting, no propagation. The semi-major axis is not stored in a TLE; it comes from the mean motion n as a = (μ/n²)^⅓ with μ = 398 600.4418 km³/s².

    Propagation

    SGP4 through satellite.js, the same analytic theory the element set was fitted to — TLEs are only meaningful with SGP4/SDP4. Positions come out in TEME, are rotated to ECEF by Greenwich mean sidereal time, then converted to geodetic sub-satellite points on WGS-84.

    Visibility

    Topocentric elevation is computed at the Bangkok site every 10 s. Each horizon crossing of the 5° mask is then bracketed and bisected to millisecond precision, so the cumulative total is not quantised by the sample step. Purely geometric — no terrain, refraction or link budget.

    The two views

    The globe is drawn in the inertial frame, where the orbit stands still and the Earth turns beneath it. The flat map is the rotating frame, where the Earth is fixed and the track walks west each revolution. Same propagation, two frames — one clock drives both.

    — SGP4 · WGS-84 · angles in degrees Element sets degrade with age; re-fetch before quoting pass times far from epoch.
